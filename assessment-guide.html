Life Choices Reflection: Assignment Guide

Complete the simulation

Complete all ten decisions: five as Kai’s parent and five as adult Kai. For each decision, briefly explain your choice. After reading the “What happens in this story” box, explain whether the result supported or challenged your expectations. Two or three sentences across the note fields for each decision are sufficient.

Part 1: Parenting reflection

Write approximately 500–650 words, excluding references. Analyze at least three parenting decisions using at least three distinct concepts from the course textbook. Explain each concept and apply it to specific observations or results. Address physical, cognitive, and social-emotional development across the reflection. Identify one choice you would reconsider and one you would retain, explaining why. Discuss context, uncertainty, continuity or change across childhood, and what you learned.

Part 2: Adult-life reflection

Write approximately 500–650 words, excluding references. Analyze at least three adult decisions using at least three distinct textbook concepts. Explain each concept and connect it to specific story evidence. Address physical, cognitive, and social-emotional development across the reflection. Identify one choice you would reconsider and one you would retain. Discuss context, uncertainty, and connections across ages. Explain what changed when you made decisions as Kai rather than as Kai’s parent.

Use evidence and sources

Use the course textbook plus at least two peer-reviewed journal articles across the assignment. Cite sources in your reflections and include an APA-formatted reference list. Both articles must contribute meaningfully; sources may support either or both reflections.

Apply concepts rather than simply naming them or retelling the story. Distinguish events shown in the simulation from possible developmental effects you predict. Consider other explanations rather than assuming one decision caused a later outcome.

Submit two files to Canvas

  1. The downloaded decision-record text file.
  2. A separate APA-formatted Word document containing both final reflections and the reference list.

You may draft your reflections inside the simulator, but you must still prepare and submit the separate Word document.

Before closing the simulator, download and open the text file to confirm that it opens correctly and includes your completed decision record. Open and check your Word document before submitting both files to Canvas. The simulator does not submit your work for you.

Grading

Please consult the rubric attached to the Life Choices Reflection assignment in Canvas for grading criteria and point values.
